BioIndicatorObservation

URI https://purl.eu/ns/air-and-water/water#BioIndicatorObservation
Definition Observation that assesses the biological Quality of Water based on the overall condition of indicator species.
Label BioIndicatorObservation
Usage note These species, also called BioIndicators, are living organisms such as plants, planktons, animals, microbes etc. More specifically, biochemical, physiological, behavioural or population changes are monitored.

BioticIndexObservation

URI https://purl.eu/ns/air-and-water/water#BioticIndexObservation
Definition Observation that assesses the biological Quality of Water on a certain scale based on what organisms are present in the Water and in what quantities.
Label BioticIndexObservation
Usage note In practice this comes down to counting specimens of certain species of macro-invertebrates like worms, snails, flies, clams etc present in the Water, weighted by the extent to which they tolerate Water pollution and combined into a single WaterqQuality score.

WaterFeature

URI https://purl.eu/ns/air-and-water/water#WaterFeature
Definition Real-world Object related to Water.
Label WaterFeature
Usage note It is the featureOfInterest, the Object of which some property is observed to assess WaterQuality. This can be for example a waterbody like the Canal Brussels-Charleroi. The Waterfeature can be typed via the "type" attribute (value would be "waterbody-canal" for example), but substitution by a typed instance (eg an instance of type "Canal", subclass of a class "Waterbody") is also allowed. In either case, the observedProperty must be a phenomenon related to the WaterFeature type. If the WaterFeature is not observed directly, but rather indirectly by sampling it, the WaterFeature is the sampledObject and the sample assumes the role of featureOfInterest.
Parent https://def.isotc211.org/iso19156/2011/GeneralFeatureInstance#GFI_Feature
